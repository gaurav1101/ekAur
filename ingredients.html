Phase 1 · The full breakdown

Everything that goes into Tulsi Nimbu Sukoon, and why.

No proprietary blends, no mystery extracts — grown in the same soil as the story. Here's what's actually in the can, ingredient by ingredient.

The calm part

Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Traditionally used in Ayurveda to support calm and steadiness through a long day. Real leaf extract, not a powdered shortcut — the same plant most Indian homes already keep on a windowsill.

Real leafnot powder extract
0mgcaffeine
The sharpness

Real lemon, not concentrate

Pressed lemon juice for the brightness — no citric-acid shortcut standing in for the real thing. It's what cuts through the tulsi and keeps this from tasting like a home remedy.

Pressed juicenot flavoring
0artificial citrus flavor
The sweetener

Jaggery, not refined sugar

Sweetened with gur — the same unrefined cane sweetener that's been in Indian kitchens for generations, made from the same crop that feeds the farmer story this brand is named after.

0refined sugar
0high-fructose corn syrup
The carbonation

Filtered water, carbonated fresh

Our base is filtered water, carbonated in small batches to a level that holds up in the can without overpowering the tulsi and lemon. Nothing exotic — just done carefully.

Small-batchcarbonation
0caffeine, always
What's not in here

The short "no" list

No caffeine. No artificial sweeteners like aspartame or sucralose. No artificial colors. No synthetic stimulants. No high-fructose corn syrup. If it's not on this list, it's not in the can.

What's coming next
