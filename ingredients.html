The full breakdown

Everything that goes into a can, and why.

No proprietary blends, no mystery flavorings — grown in the same soil as the story. Here's what's actually in Ek Aur, ingredient by ingredient.

The gut part

Chicory root fiber

The prebiotic backbone of every can. Chicory root is naturally high in inulin, a fiber your gut bacteria feed on directly — it's not digested by you, it's digested by them. That's the difference between a soda that just tastes fine and one that's actually doing something.

The sweetener

Fruit juice, not corn syrup

Every flavor is sweetened with concentrated fruit juice — mango, guava, or jamun, depending on the can — plus a small amount of cane sugar to round it out. No high-fructose corn syrup, no sugar alcohols, no aftertaste to explain away.

The heat

Real chili, real cardamom

Mango Chili gets its kick from a light chili extract steeped alongside the mango juice — not a chemical "heat" additive. Rose Cardamom uses cracked green cardamom and food-grade rose water, the same ingredients you'd find in a spice tin, just carbonated.

The carbonation

Filtered water, carbonated fresh

Our base is filtered water, carbonated in small batches to a level that holds up in the can but doesn't overpower the fruit. Nothing exotic here — just done carefully.

What's not in here

The short "no" list

No high-fructose corn syrup. No artificial sweeteners like aspartame or sucralose. No artificial colors. No preservatives beyond what carbonation and citric acid naturally provide. If it's not on this list, it's not in the can.
