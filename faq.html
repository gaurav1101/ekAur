Ingredients & health

Does Ek Aur have caffeine?+
No — Tulsi Nimbu Sukoon, and every flavor planned after it, is caffeine-free. The idea is steady, calm energy rather than a stimulant jolt and the crash that follows.
What does tulsi actually do?+
Tulsi (Holy Basil) is traditionally used in Ayurveda to support calm and steadiness. We're not making medical claims here — just using an ingredient with a long, well-known history in Indian households, at a real dose rather than a token amount.
Is it sweetened with sugar?+
It's sweetened with jaggery (gur), not refined sugar or high-fructose corn syrup, and there are no artificial sweeteners either.
Is it vegan and gluten-free?+
Yes to both. Nothing in Tulsi Nimbu Sukoon is derived from animal products or contains gluten-based ingredients.
Is this safe to drink every day?+
For most healthy adults, yes — but as with any herb-based product, if you're pregnant, breastfeeding, on medication, or managing a health condition, it's worth checking with a doctor first. We'll always list every ingredient plainly so you can make that call yourself.

Storage & serving

How should I store unopened cans?+
Room temperature is fine for storage, but Ek Aur tastes best cold. Chill for at least two hours before opening for the best carbonation and flavor.
How long does an opened can last?+
Like any carbonated drink, it's best finished the same day. Refrigerated and resealed, it'll hold most of its fizz for about 24 hours.
