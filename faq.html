Ingredients & health

What does "prebiotic" actually mean?+
Prebiotics are a type of fiber that your body doesn't digest — instead, they feed the beneficial bacteria already living in your gut. Each can of Ek Aur has 3g of prebiotic fiber from chicory root, the same source used in most prebiotic sodas and many fiber supplements.
Is Ek Aur actually low sugar?+
Yes — 5g of sugar per can, sourced from fruit juice rather than high-fructose corn syrup. For comparison, a regular soda typically has around 39g. We don't use artificial sweeteners to hit that number.
Are the flavors spicy?+
Only Mango Chili has any heat, and it's a warm finish rather than a burn — closer to a mango with a chili salt rim than a hot sauce. Rose Cardamom, Jamun Fizz, and Guava Lime have no heat at all.
Is it vegan and gluten-free?+
Yes to both. Nothing in any Ek Aur flavor is derived from animal products or contains gluten-based ingredients.

Storage & serving

How should I store unopened cans?+
Room temperature is fine for storage, but Ek Aur tastes best cold. Chill for at least two hours before opening for the best carbonation and flavor.
How long does an opened can last?+
Like any carbonated drink, it's best finished the same day. Refrigerated and resealed, it'll hold most of its fizz for about 24 hours.
